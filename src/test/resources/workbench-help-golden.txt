Timing is .pickleball/runs/<id>/launch-timing.txt. A cache hit skips Maven. Stderr timing only when PKB_WORKBENCH_TIMING=1 or -Dpkb_workbench_timing=true.
Do not pass pkb_run_profile. Do not open the GUI to test yourself. isolate is the headless session.
