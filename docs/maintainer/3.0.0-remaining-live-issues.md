## Environment limits

Not product failures. The tests skip. They were not turned into passes.

- Microsoft Edge is not on `PATH` (`microsoft-edge`, `msedge`). `apt-cache search microsoft-edge` returned no package, and apt in this sandbox cannot complete a normal Chrome-style install. `LiveEdgeSnapshotTest.liveEdgeSnapshotWhenTheBinaryIsPresent` and `SnapshotCoverageTest.liveEdgeWhenPresent` skip with `skipped: microsoft-edge is not on PATH`. `LiveEdgeSnapshotTest.edgeTokenInTheVersionStringIsEdge` passed without the binary (phase 6).
- Linux AT-SPI libraries are installed (`at-spi2-common` 2.46.0-5, `libatspi2.0-0`). `DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS` is unset. No session bus was started, because a fake bus would turn the skip into a failed live walk. `LiveDesktopTest.liveAttachSucceedsWhenAccessibilityBusIsUp` and `LiveDesktopSnapshotTest.liveSnapshotWritesJsonWhenTheBusAndDisplayExist` skip with `no AT-SPI bus`. `SnapshotCoverageTest.liveDesktopWhenThePlatformExists` skips with `AT-SPI unavailable: no session bus (DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS is unset)`.
